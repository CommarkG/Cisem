Responsive Situations — visibility per breakpoint

Three states per item, per breakpoint: visible shown directly · more collapsed under a "more" affordance (today: the single Index dropdown itself, which already acts as the compact/collapsed form on every breakpoint) · not-available not shown at all. Current reading: every item is reachable via the Index dropdown at all three breakpoints, so Desktop/Tablet show it as directly visible-in-dropdown and Mobile shows it collapsed under that same dropdown (the existing "more" pattern) — no item is presently NOT-AVAILABLE at any breakpoint. Governor may assign per-item exceptions.
