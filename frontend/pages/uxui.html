UX/UI Platform DNA

CISEM's UX/UI is platform DNA, not decoration. Two complementary poles — UX (how it feels to think here) and UI (how it's built) — held together (A9 optimal synergy). Switch tabs below; both carry the full toolbar (theme, language, search, Rows/Window view, collapse/expand, Export) — the same abilities as every other tree page in this index.
