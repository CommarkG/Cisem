# North Star Principle — Goal + Role define the essence (nested local/global)

## Governor's articulation (2026-07-22, essence preserved)
"If we define the GOAL and the ROLE of something, it gives direction for the essence of any element, including a cause
line [= corespine]. The first thing about a cause line is to define what the goal is and what the role of this issue/topic
is. Once defined, it becomes the NORTH STAR that everything relates to. We should do it in several LEVELS: take the
PLATFORM's goal — each element's goal and role should be SERVING it. There's a North Star and a LOCAL North Star. Consolidate
this into a workable principle."

## Consolidated (Opus)
**Goal + Role = the North Star.** Every element (corespine, node, page, mechanism) is defined FIRST by its Goal (what it
exists to achieve) + its Role (what it IS / does in the whole). Together they fix the essence and become the element's
North Star — the fixed reference that gives DIRECTION to every downstream decision about it (stages, elements, invariants,
wiring). Define these before anything else; without them, the rest has no direction.

**North Stars nest.** The platform's Goal+Role = the GLOBAL North Star. Every element has a LOCAL North Star (its own
Goal+Role) that must SERVE the global (and its parent corespine's). Alignment = local serves global; DRIFT = a local goal
that doesn't. The North Star flows DOWN the corespine; each level's goal serves the level above.

**Workable rule.** (1) The FIRST authored content of any course/corespine is its Goal + Role — before invariants, before
wiring. (2) Each states in one line HOW it serves its parent/global North Star. (3) Every element below traces back to
serving its local North Star, which serves the global.

- **Sharpens Gate 0** (CS-THRESHOLD-001: "goal is prior to everything") → "Goal + ROLE = North Star, and North Stars NEST."
