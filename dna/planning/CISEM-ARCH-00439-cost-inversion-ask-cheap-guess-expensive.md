## The Root (Governor's sentence, engraved)
*"In AI systems, guessing does not happen from lack of knowledge. It happens because ASKING is perceived as EXPENSIVE and GUESSING as CHEAP."* → The fix is **economic, not epistemic**: change the relative cost. Raising prohibitions (the default reflex) RAISES the cost of the honest output and makes guessing more attractive (the self-defeating-severity finding). Invert instead.
